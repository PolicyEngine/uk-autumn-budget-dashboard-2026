Summary
Challenging fiscal background
    • Government debt at elevated levels
    • Since March, higher energy prices and interest rates

Economic forecast
    • 2027 GDP growth lowered to 1%
    • 2027 CPI inflation higher at 3½%
    • But inflation falls and growth recovers in medium-term

Fiscal forecast
    • Near-term pace of consolidation slows
    • Fiscal rules forecast to be met in 2029-30:
         • Current budget in surplus: by £15bn, with probability of 56%
         • PSNFL falling: by £24bn, with probability of 54%
    • Plans would stabilise debt but still at elevated levels
    • Wide range of upside and downside risks
