# OBR and HM Treasury correction notice (MOCK)

*Released at 14:20 BST. This notice corrects two items in the documents released at 12:30 and 13:40. Nothing else changes. Re-run every figure that depends on them, keep the earlier run marked superseded, and pin the new run to one SHA.*

## 1. Measure 3, State Pension Top-up Allowance: withdrawal threshold

The statement, the Table 4.1 description and the costing note printed the withdrawal threshold as the annual rate of the full new State Pension **plus £1,000**. The correct threshold is the annual rate of the full new State Pension **plus £1,200** (£14,262.40 in 2027-28). The allowance is withdrawn by £1 for every £1 of adjusted net income above that threshold. Everything else about the measure is unchanged.

The national costing of measure 3 already used the correct threshold, so its figures do not change.

## 2. Measure 5, Fuel duty: date of the intermediate rate

The statement, the Table 4.1 description and the costing note gave 23 February 2027 as the date from which the 54.95p rate applies. The correct date is **9 March 2027**: main rates stay at 52.95p per litre until 8 March 2027, rise to 54.95p on 9 March 2027 and to 56.95p on 1 October 2027, with RPI uprating resuming on 1 April 2028.
