## 1. Annex A: Local Housing Allowance baseline

Annex A printed the frozen 2-bedroom Local Housing Allowance rate for the Central Greater Manchester Broad Rental Market Area as £1,000.00 a month. The correct frozen rate is **£985.00 a month**. The April 2027 rate under measure 8 (£1,095.00 a month) is unchanged. The national costing of measure 8 already used the correct rate and does not change.

## 2. Measure 3, Warm Home Discount top-up: caseload and costing
