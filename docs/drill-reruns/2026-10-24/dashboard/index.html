Drill reruns: drill day vs policyengine 6.10.2 MOCK

Mock-Budget drills 1–4 rerun on 24 October 2026 on the certified Microcosm. Rehearsal measures, not a real Budget.
Median and max are absolute changes in the size of each measure, over material cells (drill-day revenue at least £0.01bn), excluding invalid fuel duty rows. Closer/further compares each figure with the official static costing in the drill's filled sheet.
Fuel duty rows in drills 1–3 are not valid reruns. Those drills set fuel duty with month-dated ranges, which pe-uk 2.132.6 ignores, so only the pump-VAT part is scored (drills 2 and 3 fall by 82.9% in every year). Drill 4 used open-ended steps and is unaffected.
Decile 1 is not comparable across pe-uk 2.132.4. That version resets negative HBAI income to zero and puts those households in decile 1 instead of excluding them, so decile 1 moves even where revenue does not (open a row to see deciles).
