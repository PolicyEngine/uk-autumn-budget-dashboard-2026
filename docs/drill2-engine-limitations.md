# Accepted engine limitations: 2.100.0 versus 2.120.0
Evidence checked 5 October 2026. The selected dataset's build and release manifests both pin policyengine-uk==2.100.0 and policyengine-core==3.32.5; 2.120.0 was latest in the saved [PyPI metadata](https://pypi.org/pypi/policyengine-uk/json). Keep the requested dataset-compatible pin; describe omissions rather than silently upgrading or backporting unrelated policy changes.
All version links below target the official [2.120.0 release commit b5b0923](https://github.com/PolicyEngine/policyengine-uk/tree/b5b092312df7aa56a4e89c4b9b7c133507dea114); conclusions are release-note evidence, not newly executed model comparisons. Upstream numerical examples do not estimate impacts on the chosen dataset.

## Bugs and baseline corrections omitted by 2.100.0
- [2.100.1](https://github.com/PolicyEngine/policyengine-uk/blob/b5b092312df7aa56a4e89c4b9b7c133507dea114/CHANGELOG.md#L548): stops double uprating domestic/electricity/gas consumption already calibrated at FY26/27 prices. Upstream reports projected spending about 8% lower; chosen-data effect unmeasured. Relevant to energy/VAT baselines.
- [2.102.1](https://github.com/PolicyEngine/policyengine-uk/blob/b5b092312df7aa56a4e89c4b9b7c133507dea114/CHANGELOG.md#L523): restores Class 4's additional band at non-round thresholds, fixes regulation-100 applicability/Class 2 treatment, and prevents zero-main-rate NaNs. [Issue 1878](https://github.com/PolicyEngine/policyengine-uk/issues/1878), [fix PR 1884](https://github.com/PolicyEngine/policyengine-uk/pull/1884). Round frozen thresholds mitigate the identified rounding exposure, not every annual-maximum or zero-rate defect.
- [2.102.2](https://github.com/PolicyEngine/policyengine-uk/blob/b5b092312df7aa56a4e89c4b9b7c133507dea114/CHANGELOG.md#L516): stops deducting employee Class 1 NI from Class 4 trading profits; mixed employment/self-employment liabilities remain affected by the old engine even with frozen thresholds.
- [2.102.3](https://github.com/PolicyEngine/policyengine-uk/blob/b5b092312df7aa56a4e89c4b9b7c133507dea114/CHANGELOG.md#L509): rebases absolute-poverty reference to HBAI FYE2025 from FYE2022. It changes absolute, not relative, poverty definitions; the earlier relative-median fix 2.99.2 is already included in 2.100.0.
- [2.102.5–2.102.6](https://github.com/PolicyEngine/policyengine-uk/blob/b5b092312df7aa56a4e89c4b9b7c133507dea114/CHANGELOG.md#L488): allow pension-age families' new Housing Benefit claims; charge employer NI above State Pension age (employee exemption remains).
- [2.104.0](https://github.com/PolicyEngine/policyengine-uk/blob/b5b092312df7aa56a4e89c4b9b7c133507dea114/CHANGELOG.md#L470): fixes float32 marginal-rate precision for large incomes/gains, relevant to behavioural/CGT scoring; separate BADR elasticity is an optional new feature, off by default.
- [2.104.1 and 2.104.7](https://github.com/PolicyEngine/policyengine-uk/blob/b5b092312df7aa56a4e89c4b9b7c133507dea114/CHANGELOG.md#L421): UC counts omitted contributory ESA, Maternity Allowance, industrial injuries, Scottish carer payment and State Pension as unearned income. The old model can overstate UC for these recipients.
- [2.104.2](https://github.com/PolicyEngine/policyengine-uk/blob/b5b092312df7aa56a4e89c4b9b7c133507dea114/CHANGELOG.md#L456): fixes Pension Credit guarantee/savings-credit passporting and income/capital assessment for pensioner Council Tax Reduction.
- [2.104.3](https://github.com/PolicyEngine/policyengine-uk/blob/b5b092312df7aa56a4e89c4b9b7c133507dea114/CHANGELOG.md#L449): removes duplicate contributory JSA in household benefits/net/gross income; the old baseline overcounts it.
- [2.104.4–2.104.6](https://github.com/PolicyEngine/policyengine-uk/blob/b5b092312df7aa56a4e89c4b9b7c133507dea114/CHANGELOG.md#L428): restore structural Reform-class/tuple constructors; handle UNKNOWN-region rent uprating; avoid in-place writes into cached arrays in scenario modifiers. Dict reform behavior is unchanged by 2.104.4.
- [2.104.8 and 2.112.0](https://github.com/PolicyEngine/policyengine-uk/blob/b5b092312df7aa56a4e89c4b9b7c133507dea114/CHANGELOG.md#L414): first stop NI domestic rates following England's growth, then apply NI-specific poundage growth. These affect projected NI household bills.
- [2.105.1](https://github.com/PolicyEngine/policyengine-uk/blob/b5b092312df7aa56a4e89c4b9b7c133507dea114/CHANGELOG.md#L400): applies Housing Benefit's LHA rent cap before taper/non-dependant deductions rather than after.
- [2.106.0; clarification 2.109.2](https://github.com/PolicyEngine/policyengine-uk/blob/b5b092312df7aa56a4e89c4b9b7c133507dea114/CHANGELOG.md#L389): counts carer allowances in Pension Credit income and separates the Scottish supplement so PC/HB exclude it; new variable needed for the total Scottish carer payment.
- [2.106.1](https://github.com/PolicyEngine/policyengine-uk/blob/b5b092312df7aa56a4e89c4b9b7c133507dea114/CHANGELOG.md#L382): freezes PT, UEL, ST, LPL, UPL through 2030/31 and corrects 2025/26 LEL. [Issue 1879](https://github.com/PolicyEngine/policyengine-uk/issues/1879), [PR 1959](https://github.com/PolicyEngine/policyengine-uk/pull/1959). Issue 1879 confirms ST already held through 2030; all-four local PT/UEL/LPL/UPL baseline mitigation targets the missing freezes. LEL and Class 2 small-profits threshold are not frozen.
- [2.107.0](https://github.com/PolicyEngine/policyengine-uk/blob/b5b092312df7aa56a4e89c4b9b7c133507dea114/CHANGELOG.md#L354): extensive claimant/partner and programme-specific child definitions; fixes older dependants mistaken for partners, Marriage Allowance transfers, childcare/legacy-benefit rules, pensioner eligibility and whose income/capital counts. Broad family-structure limitation of the old baseline.
- [2.108.0](https://github.com/PolicyEngine/policyengine-uk/blob/b5b092312df7aa56a4e89c4b9b7c133507dea114/CHANGELOG.md#L342): removes actual interest/dividends/rent from UC unearned income (capital tariff remains); stops trading allowance being deducted on top of already-netted expenses.
- [2.109.0](https://github.com/PolicyEngine/policyengine-uk/blob/b5b092312df7aa56a4e89c4b9b7c133507dea114/CHANGELOG.md#L330): corrects State Pension triple-lock input timing/rounding/forecasts; upstream 2027–34 levels about 0.48% higher, provisional 2027 input. Earnings-path guarantee is new, off by default.
- [2.109.1 and 2.109.4](https://github.com/PolicyEngine/policyengine-uk/blob/b5b092312df7aa56a4e89c4b9b7c133507dea114/CHANGELOG.md#L302): fixes mixed-age UC benefit-cap/CTR pensioner treatment and Pension Credit transitional protection/UC exclusion. [2.109.3](https://github.com/PolicyEngine/policyengine-uk/blob/b5b092312df7aa56a4e89c4b9b7c133507dea114/CHANGELOG.md#L309) corrects claimant/partner capital attribution for PC.
- [2.109.6](https://github.com/PolicyEngine/policyengine-uk/blob/b5b092312df7aa56a4e89c4b9b7c133507dea114/CHANGELOG.md#L288): replaces calendar-average fuel duty with dated statutory rates weighted over fiscal years plus April RPI; annual fuel baseline changes. The runbook's explicit monthly/fiscal proration remains an accepted approximation, not this engine backport.
- [2.109.7 and 2.111.1](https://github.com/PolicyEngine/policyengine-uk/blob/b5b092312df7aa56a4e89c4b9b7c133507dea114/CHANGELOG.md#L277): fix full Housing Benefit income/capital passporting for IS/JSA-IB/ESA-IR; correct earnings-only disregards, fixed statutory amounts and work conditions.
- [2.109.8](https://github.com/PolicyEngine/policyengine-uk/blob/b5b092312df7aa56a4e89c4b9b7c133507dea114/CHANGELOG.md#L262): fixes optional UC two-child age-exemption reform, dynamics/cliff eligibility and 2023 minimum wage; core>=3.32.12 improves branch memory without changing results.
- [2.110.0, 2.111.2, 2.112.0–2.112.1, 2.113.5, 2.117.0](https://github.com/PolicyEngine/policyengine-uk/blob/b5b092312df7aa56a4e89c4b9b7c133507dea114/CHANGELOG.md#L251): successive fixes to overlapping carer payments, entitlement versus payment, carer/severe-disability additions and premiums, non-dependants and cross-family carer attribution, and per-person carer premiums. 2.117.0 also corrects 2025/26 weekly carer premium.
- [2.111.0](https://github.com/PolicyEngine/policyengine-uk/blob/b5b092312df7aa56a4e89c4b9b7c133507dea114/CHANGELOG.md#L240): UC childcare partner exceptions and treated-as-working conditions; new inputs for job offers/temporary absence.
- [2.112.0](https://github.com/PolicyEngine/policyengine-uk/blob/b5b092312df7aa56a4e89c4b9b7c133507dea114/CHANGELOG.md#L199): birth-date-specific State Pension age, including 66→67 during 2026–28, and related pension classification; pensioner CTR earnings disregards; improved parent/adult-child inference. Old male/female age parameters are replaced only in newer engines.
- [2.112.1](https://github.com/PolicyEngine/policyengine-uk/blob/b5b092312df7aa56a4e89c4b9b7c133507dea114/CHANGELOG.md#L188): Class 4 profits after capital/trading allowances and trade losses; year-specific loss carry-forward and simulation-clone input provenance. Frozen thresholds do not supply these fixes.
- [2.113.0](https://github.com/PolicyEngine/policyengine-uk/blob/b5b092312df7aa56a4e89c4b9b7c133507dea114/CHANGELOG.md#L181): statutory rent/CTR apportionment for sharers, lodgers/boarders, separate HB LHA category and non-dependant rules; substantive new coverage absent from 2.100.0.
- [2.113.2–2.113.3](https://github.com/PolicyEngine/policyengine-uk/blob/b5b092312df7aa56a4e89c4b9b7c133507dea114/CHANGELOG.md#L159): UC deducts only each claimant's own employment-related tax/NI; fixes duplicate projected additional State Pension; includes Scottish carer payment in CTR income.
- [2.114.1–2.114.3](https://github.com/PolicyEngine/policyengine-uk/blob/b5b092312df7aa56a4e89c4b9b7c133507dea114/CHANGELOG.md#L106): fixes savings-credit-only HB assessment, childcare/student-support eligibility/payment rules, and published LHA determinations/monthly UC rates plus genuine Scottish rent distributions.
- [2.115.0](https://github.com/PolicyEngine/policyengine-uk/blob/b5b092312df7aa56a4e89c4b9b7c133507dea114/CHANGELOG.md#L90): Class 4 exemption uses pension age at tax-year start rather than 6 October; separates pension-credit qualifying age and correct programme pension-age routes. Historical men's qualifying-age difference affects 2018/19 and earlier.
- [2.117.1–2.117.2](https://github.com/PolicyEngine/policyengine-uk/blob/b5b092312df7aa56a4e89c4b9b7c133507dea114/CHANGELOG.md#L46): UC passporting in HB (release states no current Enhanced-FRS output change); assigns CTR to liable household-head family, preventing duplicate awards to tied oldest adults.
- [2.118.0](https://github.com/PolicyEngine/policyengine-uk/blob/b5b092312df7aa56a4e89c4b9b7c133507dea114/CHANGELOG.md#L31): UC minimum-income-floor net calculation, claimant-only/couple rules, age minimum wage and trading-loss treatment; material self-employment baseline limitation.
- [2.119.0–2.120.0](https://github.com/PolicyEngine/policyengine-uk/blob/b5b092312df7aa56a4e89c4b9b7c133507dea114/CHANGELOG.md#L1): precise birth-date cutoffs for UC/CTC/PC child rules and PC mixed-age saving; UC income, cap and childcare conditions use only assessed claimants, excluding dependants' income/work.
